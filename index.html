Headline findings

  • Global mean adult BMI rises from 25.0 kg/m² (2015) to 27.7 kg/m² (2050, SSP2-4.5); global obesity prevalence nearly doubles (14.4% → ~30.7%).
  • High-BMI-attributable deaths and DALYs increase in all four scenarios; scenario differences at the burden level are small because disease baselines are shared across SSPs.
  • Attributable burden is increasingly concentrated in low- and middle-income regions, with marked spatial heterogeneity at sub-national scale.
